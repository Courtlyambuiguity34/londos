# ADR-0012: measured resource snapshots and reported local operating costs

P9.9 development packet, 2026-10-06, UTC. Preserve applied migrations 001–015, prior privacy/financial/audit boundaries and independent jobs. This packet reports fictional loopback resources; it does not bill merchants, configure providers or authorize live expenses.

Fresh current owners see only their shop's aggregate media/queue/reporting summary. A new explicit costs grant and native fresh MFA permit bounded platform aggregates and a minimal paginated shop selection queue. Private journal detail/commands additionally require a distinct claimed `operator_cost_scopes`, no active merchant self-membership, reason and confirmation. No analytics/support/review/finance/operations/privacy scope substitutes. Queries and commands recheck authority after merchant/user serialization and after aggregate work. Reports contain no byte payloads, storage keys, job payloads, credential values, other members' identities or unrelated investigation records. Owner summaries exclude operator identities/private reasons and private application/trust evidence volume.

Current stored media bytes are measured from canonical blob lengths and compared with existing recorded quota metadata. Asset state counts, variant counts and reserved quota are current snapshots; reserved bytes are headroom, not stored bytes. Physical database overhead, backups/WAL, filesystem/provider replication and bandwidth are unavailable. Platform evidence bytes are separate aggregate totals; pre-shop application bytes are not arbitrarily assigned to a shop, and costs authority grants no evidence viewer.

Job use comes from current tenant-consistent queue rows and their state/attempt counters, without reading payloads. Attempt counters can reset on existing authorized replay; they are not lifetime executions or duration/CPU measures. Shared merchant/platform jobs remain separate from shop-bound work and are never distributed by invented estimates. Existing queue/worker behavior is unchanged. AI is not implemented and no provider charge source exists; missing sources are reported as unavailable rather than zero cost. Wall-clock backlog age is not staff working time.

A separate append-only development journal records operator-reported support, verification or rent-collection minutes and UGX expense minor units for a specific canonical shop and UTC work day. There is no guessed hourly rate, conversion, combined unit or automatic financial charge. Manual entries are reported, not independently provider-verified measurements. Each entry has an immutable retry key/fingerprint; unchanged retries return the original result and changed retries conflict. Current journal versions serialize concurrent entries. Reasoned corrections append an exact linked reversal; one reversal per original and same tenant/shop/category/day prevent double cancellation or widening. Original records remain immutable. Current net summaries deduct reversals against their original work day, including later correction dates. Retried correction cannot credit rent or alter invoice, tenancy, publication, moderation, operations, ownership or privacy holds.

Windows are 7/30/90 UTC calendar days including today. Manual reporting accepts a day in the last 90 days, integer minutes 0–480, integer UGX minor expenses 0–1,000,000,000 and at least one nonzero quantity. At most 100 original entries per shop per UTC creation day are accepted, independent of session timezone; exact corrections remain available after that limit. Zero net reported amounts mean only that the recorded journal sums to zero; completeness is unknown. No entries means unmeasured reporting, not free operation. Private journal pages contain at most 20 entries and cursor navigation; platform queue pages contain at most 20 minimal shops. Aggregate statements have a two-second timeout and fixed output shapes. Existing canonical rows are read; no old measurements/time records are invented at upgrade. Missing quota metadata is unavailable, not a recorded zero.

The [source inventory and evidence](../evidence/X2-P9-COSTS.md) distinguish logical measurements from reported work and absent price/time sources. Immutable reported history has no automatic retention deletion; approved production retention/privacy and least-privilege database roles remain release obligations. No worker, queue, financial or privacy effect behavior changes in this packet.

Acceptance requires additive schema-015 preservation, exact byte/attribution/counter tests, independent scope/native MFA, fresh owner isolation, lock-wait revocation, strict inputs, integer units, immutable history, concurrent retry/correction/version checks, actual journal/query failure containment, and accessible native desktop/mobile workflows. Main/local privacy erasure and all live collection/analytics/notifications remain disabled. P9.7's locally measurable cost views and P9.9 development reporting can complete at this scope; live provider/working-time validation and P9.8 legal/backup/full subject-access remain explicit gates. P10 AI, wider LSS/public pagination and release/R2 work follow.
