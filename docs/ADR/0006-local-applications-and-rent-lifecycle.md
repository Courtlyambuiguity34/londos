# ADR-0006: local applications and manually reconciled rent

P8 implementation contract, 2026-10-05. The owner selected a configurable **development** policy: UGX 5,000 per 30 days, seven days' grace and manual operator reconciliation. Live collection stays disabled. Policy versions are immutable; obligations retain their selected version. This is not approval of launch pricing, tax/fee treatment, legal retention or a payment provider.

Authenticated owners create/edit/submit applications with bounded business details and private normalized image evidence. New applicants can establish their own merchant membership atomically. Staff cannot submit or review an application on an owner's behalf. Submitted details are frozen until a reviewer requests information. Operators need current review permission and real MFA; claiming a minimal queue entry creates an audited application scope. Detailed records/evidence require that scope. Approval/rejection/requests for information require a reason, fresh authentication and the current version. Self-review/reconciliation is denied. Duplicate business signals are advisory, never automatic rejection.

Approval issues the first referenced invoice before a shop exists. Only a finance operator with an explicitly claimed application scope and fresh MFA may record a development payment or waiver. Merchant claims and browser success URLs have no settlement authority. Partial credits are supported, overpayments and currency/reference mismatches are rejected without effects. Exact integer minor units and append-only events are required. Stable command keys make retries idempotent; reusing a key with a different payload is a conflict. A receipt reference cannot credit two obligations. Reversals append an equal negative correction linked once to the original credit, with a reason; they never edit/delete the original event.

An invoice's initial dates are provisional until its first full settlement. At that point its coverage is finalized once, for the policy duration, starting at the later of settlement time and the previous coverage end. The financial amount/currency/reference and finalized coverage are immutable. Partial payment does not activate or extend coverage. Full first settlement, finalization, immutable shop identity, capacity allocation, unpublished shop/tenancy/moderation records, audit and notifications commit in one transaction. A rollback leaves no partial shop. Capacity allocation is serialized across merchants and creates another logical mall when existing planning thresholds are reached.

Renewal creates one open obligation at a time. Reconciliation and scheduler work serialize on the merchant and reread current canonical events; old jobs cannot close a just-renewed shop. A reversal creates arrears and closes tenancy while preserving catalogue/design/history; repaying the reversed amount restores eligibility only within valid coverage. Renewal never changes moderation or publishes a draft. Active coverage ends at its finalized UTC instant; the first overdue day is `due`, the rest of seven days is `grace`, and the exact grace boundary is `closed`. Grace zero closes at expiry. These fixed-day development intervals avoid inventing calendar-month or DST policy.

Scheduled local reminders are deduplicated, bounded and durable. The worker uses its current trusted clock, not dates supplied by a browser or old job payload. Tests inject time into server-only service calls. Only contracts created by P8 enter automatic scheduling; existing fixture shops/tenancies are not silently converted. Public visibility continues to use the canonical P6/P7 rule, with no shared response cache. Financial history, scopes, private evidence and lifecycle endpoints require dedicated tenant, fresh-auth, race, retry/reversal and browser evidence before this packet is marked verified.

Local image evidence uses the existing strict JPEG/PNG/WebP decoder, strips metadata and stores only a bounded normalized variant privately. No PDF, remote URL or raw download is supported. Two images per application and version-checked replacement limit storage; historical decisions retain audit metadata, not publicly downloadable identity material. Live verification/retention policy and production encrypted object storage remain later gates.
