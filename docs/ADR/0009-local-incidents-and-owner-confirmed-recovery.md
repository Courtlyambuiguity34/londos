# ADR-0009: local incident ownership and owner-confirmed recovery

P9.3/P9.4 local packet, 2026-10-05. Preserve ADR-0008 trust cases and all independent financial/publication/operational state. This is a fictional loopback workflow, not live identity adjudication or a password/MFA bypass. Analytics and governed retention/export/deletion remain separate P9 work.

Fresh shop owners open bounded, idempotent incidents for security, account recovery, contact/payment-instruction change, fraud, payment exceptions, verification or support. The reporter is derived from the authenticated actor, never a supplied user ID. Only a contact-change incident references a requested earlier canonical contact revision; immutable history begins with the current upgrade baseline and captures subsequent changes. Earlier history cannot be reconstructed or claimed verified.

Minimal prioritized queues filter by the kind's existing explicit operator grant: recovery for security/account/contact incidents, moderation for fraud, finance for payment exceptions, review for verification, support for support. Native MFA is mandatory. Fresh reasoned claims assign one responsible operator and a distinct incident scope; other shop/application/trust scopes do not confer it. Self-membership denies investigation. Case details/commands require fresh MFA, current assignment and scope, rechecked after the merchant lock. Version races have one winner. Reasoned escalation relinquishes assignment and scopes so another qualified reviewer can claim; escalation needs human ownership and is not a live response SLA.

Private investigation notes are separate from owner-facing explanations. Confirmed notes/priority, escalation, resolution and containment append immutable events/audit, plus minimal local owner notices where appropriate. Closure/cancellation never erases history or reverses money/moderation/holds. Fraud/finance/verification incidents provide triage and links to the existing separately scoped decision workflows, not new powers over those records.

Recovery reviewers may contain the reporting active owner's account once per eligible case by rotating its existing credential generation, revoking all sessions/proofs/pending MFA challenges and outstanding native password-reset tokens atomically. Native authentication's generation checks fence in-flight old sign-in/MFA completions. Revocation is enforced at the existing final authentication check; already-authorized commands are not promised retroactive cancellation. This does not change passwords, email, factors, roles or memberships, or permanently block future sign-in. The owner must use native password reset/change and existing MFA/recovery codes. Lost-all-factor/ownership/email adjudication requires a separately approved identity-proof policy and remains unavailable; ordinary support cannot perform containment or sensitive corrections.

A contact restoration is limited to the exact immutable earlier revision selected by the incident's reporter. After investigation, that same currently active owner must explicitly confirm with fresh native authentication, current case/contact versions and a reason. Approval expires after 30 minutes and binds the owner's credential generation. Containment/password reset invalidates it. The assigned recovery operator requires fresh MFA, matching case/current-contact versions, unexpired approval, unchanged generation and still-active owner authority before restoring. A later contact edit requires renewed owner confirmation. The old snapshot is validated using the current contact schema, restored as a new canonical version and retained in history; no operator-supplied replacement fields are accepted. Notifications/audit are committed atomically. Finance ledger/payment collection is unaffected.

Owner pages require fresh owner access before HTML/Flight and expose redacted investigation history. Only the original reporter can confirm restoration; current owners may cancel an open own-shop incident. Operator pages and private APIs require their own incident authority. Messages/old contact values, credentials and investigation reasons never enter public rendering, queue metadata, logs or notification payloads. No evidence upload or unrestricted user browser is introduced. Explicit guarded local setup may grant recovery/support to the known fictional operator, preserving default seeds and credentials; native MFA and case claims still apply.

Acceptance requires additive upgrade preservation, immutable contact/case/history, two merchants, staff/support denial, grant/scope/assignment isolation, native MFA, owner confirmation expiry/generation fencing, real lock-wait revocation, competing assignments/restores, atomic session containment with a native in-flight old-password sign-in test and rejection of previously issued reset tokens, HTTP/Flight attacks and accessible desktop/mobile journeys. Live escalation, identity recovery, legal retention and production release remain separate gates.
