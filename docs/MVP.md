# MVP & Validation Roadmap

## Goal

Prove that merchants and customers value the **digital mall / rented shop** model before building every possible feature.

## First validation cohort

Target roughly **10–20 real merchants** across a manageable set of categories.

The pilot should answer:
- Will merchants complete onboarding?
- Can they create an attractive shop without a developer?
- Will they share their Londos shop link?
- Do customers open those links and browse?
- Does mall search generate discovery beyond direct links?
- Do customers move from searched item → other products in the same shop?
- Which contact/commerce method actually converts?
- Will merchants renew/pay rent after experiencing the product?
- What support burden does each merchant create?
- What does one active shop cost Londos to operate?

## Smallest trustworthy product

### Customer
- fast public entrance;
- categories;
- global search;
- shop results;
- public shop;
- department/product navigation;
- direct/deep links;
- merchant-selected contact/commerce action;
- no mandatory customer login.

### Shopkeeper
- secure authentication;
- shop profile/branding;
- departments;
- product/service CRUD;
- image upload/optimization;
- basic safe shop design;
- commerce/contact settings;
- publish/unpublish;
- basic analytics;
- rent/status view.

### Landlord
- secure MFA;
- applications;
- approve/reject;
- shop ID/mall assignment;
- merchant/shop verification state;
- tenancy/rent status;
- grace/close/reopen controls;
- reports/moderation;
- basic occupancy/operations dashboard;
- audit trail.

## Can wait until evidence

Potential later capabilities:
- sophisticated AI design;
- professional designer marketplace;
- full Shop SDK;
- 3D/360 showroom tooling;
- complex recommendations;
- integrated payments/custody;
- advanced messaging;
- loyalty;
- native mobile apps;
- microservices;
- multi-region infrastructure.

“Later” does not mean “never”; it means do not let them prevent learning.

## Pilot success metrics

Track at minimum:
- merchant activation rate;
- time to publish first shop;
- active shops/week;
- direct-link visits;
- discovery/search visits;
- search-to-shop click-through;
- shop-to-contact/enquiry conversion;
- cross-department browsing;
- repeat customer visits;
- merchant renewal intent and actual paid renewal;
- support minutes per merchant;
- storage/bandwidth per merchant;
- infrastructure cost per active shop;
- report/fraud rate.

## Definition of initial success

The MVP succeeds if it demonstrates repeatable merchant onboarding, real customer engagement, evidence that shared mall discovery adds value beyond a standalone merchant link, acceptable trust/performance, and willingness to renew at economically sustainable rent.
